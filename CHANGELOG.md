# Changelog

## 1.2.0 (2026-10-30)

- New Spells tab, with spell icons, to switch off single crowd controls; stuns, fears, incapacitates, silences and the rest can each be switched off as a category.
- One Skom's minimap button opens all your Skom's addons when two or more are installed (/skoms).

## 1.1.1 (2026-10-26)

- New display name: Skom's Healer CC.

## 1.1.0 (2026-10-23)

- Healers by name for each bracket: 2v2, 3v3 and Solo Queue.
- New About tab and a dedicated settings window (`/shcc`).

## 1.0.0 (2026-10-21)

First release.

- A big icon, countdown and sound when your healer is crowd controlled, with the border colored by dispel type.
- Automatic healer detection, 152 crowd control effects.
- Arenas by default, battlegrounds optional.
